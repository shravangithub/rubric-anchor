Why GCC is separate from IT services

A services firm delivers to a client. A captive centre owns a charter.

Those need different evidence. Services rewards client-facing ownership, estimation and scoping, multi-account range. GCC rewards charter ownership, global counterpart relationships, building a function in-house, and moving work up from cost arbitrage to real ownership. Collapsing them — as I did in the first version — loses the distinction that matters most in Indian hiring.

Why frontier AI is separate from applied AI

Applied AI ships models into a product: lifecycle, evaluation, serving under latency and cost.

A frontier lab asks something else entirely — novel contribution, training at scale, distributed training systems, alignment and safety research, getting more capability from less compute. It carries the heaviest pack weight of the twelve at 17.7%, because in that context the domain evidence genuinely is most of the signal.

The honest caveat

These weights are a considered starting point, not a validated instrument.

They encode plausible relative importance, not measured predictive validity. Reweight them for your context, record who approved it and when — and once you have a few hundred hires, run graph.parameter_separation() and let the outcomes tell you which of them were ever doing any work.
